What is Liar's Cards?

Liar's Cards is a free online multiplayer bluffing card game you can play directly in your browser — no download, no install. Each player is dealt a hand of standard playing cards. On every turn you must choose: Play cards face-down (and claim they match the declared rank), Dare the previous player to reveal one of their cards, or Pass the round. The last player left holding cards loses.

How to Play — Quick Rules

  1. Start the round: The first player declares a rank (e.g. "Kings") and plays 1 or more cards face-down, claiming they are Kings. Any card can be played — truth or bluff.
  2. Your turn options: You can Play more cards (claiming the same rank), Dare the previous player by picking one card to reveal, or Pass to sit out the rest of the round.
  3. Dare result: If the revealed card matches the declared rank, the darer takes all pile cards into their hand. If it's a lie, the bluffer takes them.
  4. All-pass reset: If every active player passes, the pile is locked into an untouchable temp pile and the round restarts.
  5. Win condition: Empty your hand to win. The last player still holding cards loses.
