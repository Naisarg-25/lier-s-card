🃏 How to Play Liar's Cards
1

Every Turn: Play, Dare, or Pass

On your turn, you have three options:

  • Play: Put 1 or more cards face down, claiming they match the declared rank (e.g. "2 Kings"). Any card can be a bluff!
  • Dare: Challenge the previous player's claim! You choose 1 of their played cards (e.g. Card #1 or Card #2) to flip face up.
  • Pass: Skip this round. Once you pass, you sit out until the table pile resets.
2

Selected Card Order Strategy

You can select any cards in any custom order (not restricted to sequential ranks). When you play multiple cards, opponents will choose Card #1, Card #2, etc. to reveal. Place your bluff or truth strategically in the sequence!

3

Dare Penalty & Rewards

  • If the revealed card is TRUTHFUL: The challenger was wrong and takes all discard cards into their hand!
  • If the revealed card is a LIE: The bluffer was caught and takes all discard cards into their hand!
4

Locked Temp Pile & Winning

If all players pass, all cards in the center pile get permanently locked into the Temp Pile and cannot be picked up. Empty your hand to win — the last player holding cards loses!
