How to Play Liar's Cards

Complete rules for the online multiplayer bluffing card game — turn order, card claims, dare challenges, the temp pile, and how to win.

🎯 Game Overview

Liar's Cards is a multiplayer bluffing card game played with a standard 52-card deck. 2–6 players are each dealt an equal hand. The goal is to empty your hand first. The last player still holding cards loses the game.

Every player can tell the truth or bluff on any turn. Opponents must decide whether to believe you, challenge you with a Dare, or Pass. Reading other players — and hiding your own cards — is the core skill.

🔄 Your Three Actions Each Turn

On your turn you have exactly three choices:

🃏 PLAY Place 1 or more cards face-down and claim they match the declared rank. You can tell the truth or bluff.
🔍 DARE Challenge the previous player's claim. Pick one of their played cards to flip face-up and reveal.
🚫 PASS Skip this round. Once you pass you sit out until the current pile resets.

👑 Declaring a Rank

The first player to act in a fresh round must declare a rank — for example, "Kings" — and then play 1 or more cards claiming they are all Kings. That declared rank is locked for the entire round. All subsequent plays that round must also claim that same rank.

You are not required to actually play matching cards. You can place any card from your hand and claim it is the declared rank. That is what makes this a bluffing game.

Example

Alex goes first and declares "Kings", playing 2 cards face-down. The declared rank is now Kings for this round. Everyone after Alex must play cards claiming they are Kings, dare Alex, or pass.

🂠 Playing Cards — Truth or Bluff

When you choose to Play, select 1 or more cards from your hand and place them face-down. You are claiming they all match the current declared rank. The important strategic detail: when you play multiple cards, opponents must choose which specific card to reveal if they dare you. Play your bluff cards carefully in the sequence.

Card order matters

You choose the exact order of your played cards. Cards are revealed as Card #1, Card #2, Card #3 in that order if challenged. Place your genuine matching cards where you think opponents are most likely to look, or bury your bluffs at positions where challenges are risky.

Example

Sam plays 3 cards claiming Kings. Sam actually has 1 real King and 2 bluff cards. Sam places the real King as Card #2. If dared, opponents must pick a specific card — picking Card #1 or #3 will catch the lie; Card #2 will not.

🔍 The Dare Challenge

If you think the previous player lied, you can Dare them. You choose exactly one card from their last play to flip face-up. The result determines who gets penalised with all the cards currently in the pile:

  • If the revealed card matches the declared rank (truth): The darer was wrong. The darer takes all cards currently in the discard pile into their hand. The lied-to player goes next.
  • If the revealed card does not match (a lie): The bluffer is caught. The bluffer takes all discard pile cards into their hand. The darer goes next.
Example — Lie caught

Jordan dares Alex. Jordan picks Card #1. It's a 7 of Spades, not a King. Alex was bluffing — Alex takes all 8 pile cards. Jordan starts the next round.

Example — Truth revealed

Jordan dares Alex. Jordan picks Card #2. It's the King of Hearts — a genuine King. Jordan takes all 8 pile cards into their hand. Alex starts the next round.

Daring on a player's last card

If a player empties their hand by playing their last cards, they enter a pending finish state. The next player must Dare or Pass. If the next player dares and the last-card play was truthful, the empty-handed player wins immediately. If it was a lie, they receive all pile cards back and the game continues.

🚫 Passing

You can Pass to skip your turn without playing or daring. Once you pass in a round, you sit out that round until the pile resets. Passing is a valid tactic when you have no good cards to play and you think the previous player told the truth.

What happens when everyone passes

If all active players pass in the same round, the entire discard pile is permanently locked into an untouchable Temp Pile. Those cards can never be picked up by anyone. The player who started that round begins a fresh round and may declare any new rank.

Example

There are 12 cards in the pile. All 4 players pass in turn. The 12 cards go to the locked temp pile. The round restarter declares "5s" and play begins fresh.

🏆 Winning and Losing

  • The first player to empty their hand wins the game — provided no one dares them and proves their last play was a lie.
  • As players empty their hands they are confirmed as finished and exit active play.
  • The game continues among remaining players until only one player is left still holding cards.
  • The last player holding cards loses.

🎮 Game Modes

  • With Friends: Create a private room. Share the 6-character room code. Up to 6 players. The host starts the game and can remove players from the lobby.
  • With Randoms: Automatic matchmaking. 4-player rooms. The game starts automatically when the room fills.
  • With AI: Play solo against 1–5 AI bot opponents. Bots can Dare, Play, or Pass and make strategic decisions. Good for practice.
